# Operating-system and business vision

Version: 0.3 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Draft specification

## Mission

Turn founder ideas into enduring businesses through coordinated AI execution across the complete product and business lifecycle.

## End state

Founder / board → AI executive orchestration → departmental responsibilities → specialist agents → authorized tools → products and customers → evidence and memory → continuous improvement.

UNI has no planned human employees or routine human operators. The founder governs strategy and reserved decisions. External manufacturers, logistics providers, banks, and other vendors may employ people; their dependencies must be recorded honestly.

## Scope

Support actual businesses across software and physical goods, including the founder's LondonRue towel-business example. Hardware design, physical operations and supplier management require business-specific scope and verified capabilities. Unilogistix itself is the reusable operating framework, never an actual company.

Safe Goes is the blueprint's proposed first serious product: a GPS-tracking business. It remains separate from the UNI parent and has not received a launch mandate.

## Repeated venture creation

A business receives a dedicated instance for authorized discovery, development and operation, with isolated credentials, budget, operational memory, support ownership and configured governance. The master framework remains reusable for the next opportunity. Viability and autonomy graduation are later evidence gates, not prerequisites for an initial instance. Replication is a controlled deployment and handover, not permission to create unlimited descendants.

## Evidence of progress

Measure retained customer value, contribution economics, cash obligations, service reliability, recovery, support outcomes, and time spent waiting for routine human execution. Count manual interventions rather than assuming autonomy.

Each business instance and the master framework should become more capable without becoming less accountable.

## Founder-directed autonomy requirement

**Every operational human ask is a failure.** Ordinary work must use autonomous resolution and recovery. Count unavoidable asks, actual human execution, and unresolved operations honestly. Reserved board authority does not permit relabeling routine operational decisions. See [the autonomy policy](policies/autonomy.md).
