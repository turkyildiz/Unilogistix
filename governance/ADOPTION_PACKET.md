# Document scope and adoption packet

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft; adoption and activation pending

## Reviewable deliverable

The [ten-book library](../books/README.md) contains 307 completed draft chapters, supported by the constitution’s 60 role contracts, policies, decision rights, control register, operating records and legal applicability process. All eight gaps and 18 acceptance scenarios from the latest [reporting review](REVIEW_DISPOSITION.md) are accounted for. The source proposals remain preserved in reference.

The agreed direction is a reusable Unilogistix framework, never an actual company; independently governed actual-business instances such as LondonRue; experience-driven framework CI/CD and compatible instance upgrades; founder governance and autonomous internal work; US/Illinois as the starting business legal profile. Today’s authority covers documentation completion and GitHub publication only.

## Decisions still to record

Actual founder/governance may adopt the exact published commit and effective date, accepting or amending the detailed constitutional and ten-book rules, the completion standard in [DOCUMENT_SCOPE.md](DOCUMENT_SCOPE.md), and the eight BD-GOV proposals mapped in the review disposition. No adoption is inferred from requesting edits, supplying suggestions, or publishing the manuscript. Record scope agreement separately from detailed policy adoption in the [board register](BOARD_REGISTER.md).

The proposed one-appeal/one-business-day dispute clocks and watchdog acknowledgment/progress defaults are company policy choices, not statutory deadlines. They remain reviewable adoption terms. An authenticated adoption record identifies scope, exact version/commit, effective time, exceptions, superseded rules and authority; a generated summary is not a signature.
