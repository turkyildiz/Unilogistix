## Evidence states and decision ownership

Use **documented**, **reported test**, **observed configuration**, and **independently verified outcome** as distinct evidence types. Also record acceptance state: pending, failed, passed for exact scope, or not applicable with independently reviewed rationale. Neither an unobserved control nor a skipped required test is a pass.

R01 assembles the business readiness packet; R08 tracks dependencies and unresolved items; R07 checks authentic scope and adoption; independent specialists judge their gates; R52 audits integrity without becoming the routine transaction approver. The actual authorized business governance decides any new implementation mandate or live pilot. A failed gate stays owned and restricts the affected activity. Operational repairs use existing AI authority; routine human asks remain autonomy failures.

Keep live publication, real charges, supplier commitments and physical actions disabled. Testing infrastructure and inference still need valid resource authority. ## Pilot and broader autonomy decisions

After every applicable launch-critical gate has independently accepted evidence, actual governance may authorize a bounded pilot with specific products, markets/audience, exposure, customer volume, actions, duration, observation window, stop thresholds and continuity. “Not applicable” needs factual rationale and independent review; it cannot excuse a failed mandatory control. Use the [readiness packet template](../templates/operating-readiness.md).

A pilot does not establish sustained autonomy. Its representative volume and real observation period must cover relevant fulfillment/remedy or renewal/cancellation behavior. Synthetic time and test payments prove neither real demand nor long-run customer outcomes. Any operational human ask remains counted; repair the cause and repeat the affected qualification/observation requirement. Expand scope only on accepted evidence and authentic authority. No universal volume or time period is invented here.

A material change to business scope, offer, policy, model, prompt, credentials, provider, code, artifact or recovery invalidates affected evidence until reassessed. The readiness owner records which gates require rerun; independent specialists verify the impact assessment. Missing evidence, failed controls or exceeded stop thresholds restrict activity and create an owned repair task. Recovery never silently reverses a founder stop.
