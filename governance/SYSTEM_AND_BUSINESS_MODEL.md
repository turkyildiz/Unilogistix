# Unilogistix and the businesses it operates

Version: 0.1 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Founder-confirmed identity model; detailed operating rules proposed

## Founder direction

Unilogistix will never be an actual company. It is the reusable AI operating system
and governing framework used to develop and operate actual businesses. The founder
gave the example of LondonRue, a business selling towels: Unilogistix clones into
a LondonRue instance, which develops and operates as LondonRue.

LondonRue is used as the consistent example spelling. The example does not establish
an existing entity, legal name, ownership structure, launch decision or budget.
It establishes that the framework must support physical-goods businesses as well
as software businesses. Software-first language is no longer a universal business
scope restriction.

## Three distinct scopes

| Scope | Purpose | Authority and records |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unilogistix master framework | Reusable policies, role designs, workflows, knowledge standards and evaluated operating capabilities | Founder governs framework changes and permitted reuse; this is not a legal parent company |
| Business instance, such as LondonRue AI operations | A dedicated copy configured to develop and run one business under its identity | Business-specific mandate, brand, roles, memory, data, accounts, spending limits and reporting |
| Actual business and its responsible owner/entity | Customer and supplier relationships, assets, products and obligations | Actual legal/account ownership and signing authority must be recorded for that business; copying software does not establish these facts |

The word "parent" means the originating framework or a permitted technical
supervisory relationship unless a document explicitly identifies an actual legal
ownership relationship. The framework is not presumed to own shares, hold money,
employ people, sell products or sign contracts. Shared software does not establish
common ownership or permission to access another business's records.

## Proposed instance lifecycle

1. Record the founder's business idea, intended customers, business identity and discovery mandate.
2. Instantiate a pinned version of the framework in an isolated business scope. An instance can exist during discovery and development; profitability is not a prerequisite for creating it.
3. Configure business-specific rules, permitted markets, product model, budgets, data boundaries, accountable roles and dependencies. Keep unavailable permissions explicitly inactive.
4. Develop the business: research demand, validate products and suppliers, establish economics, prepare the storefront and operations, and define the customer journey.
5. Activate selling and other consequential actions only within that business's adopted mandate and completed readiness gates.
6. Operate and improve the business as that business: market, sell, fulfill, support, reconcile money, maintain systems and learn from outcomes.
7. Evaluate viability, autonomy and any transfer of operating responsibility using evidence. Graduation measures demonstrated maturity; it does not create the first business identity or instance.
8. Maintain the master framework for future businesses while preserving support and obligations in existing instances.

## What a clone contains

Copy reusable, versioned role definitions, operating procedures, templates,
approved generic knowledge, interfaces and evaluation specifications. Configure
the business identity and mandate separately. Enable only responsibilities needed
by the business; a department definition does not require a continuously running
worker.

Do not copy live credentials, payment destinations, customer lists, private memory,
existing authority, paid-provider allocations or another business's contractual
commitments as defaults. Any authorized transfer of assets or obligations needs
an explicit inventory, responsible parties, reconciliation and acceptance record.

## Business specialization and branding

The LondonRue instance speaks and operates as LondonRue through authorized
LondonRue channels. It must use the recorded seller and account identity where
required and represent its AI role truthfully. It cannot invent human staff,
company registration, certifications or customer reviews.

For a towel business, the operating specification covers supplier qualification,
product specifications and claims, quality evidence, landed cost, inventory,
storefront, marketing, orders, payment, delivery, returns, refunds and customer
support. AI coordinates authorized providers; it does not claim to physically
manufacture, inspect or deliver goods without evidence of those activities.

The [marketing operating model](../books/BOOK-06-BUSINESS-OPERATIONS/AUTONOMOUS-MARKETING.md)
belongs inside the business instance. Campaigns use that business's audience,
claims, channels, budget and customer outcomes. A platform-level reusable campaign
template does not authorize customer contact in any business.

## Governance and improvement

The founder explicitly confirmed that everything learned from LondonRue should
improve Unilogistix and the next business through CI/CD. The required
[learning workflow](../workflows/business-to-framework-learning.md) records evidence,
disposition and promotion into evaluated framework versions. This is part of the
confirmed scope; the detailed controls below remain proposed operating rules.

The founder controls the master framework and assigns authority for each business
instance. "Board" remains an operating-governance label; it does not assert that
Unilogistix has corporate directors or that an example business has a particular
legal form. Actual business governance and any other owners' rights must be
represented accurately in that business's schedule.

Proposed shared improvement rule: every business records learning outcomes and
contributes validated, permitted reusable lessons. Master updates are versioned proposals to existing
instances, evaluated for compatibility and released with rollback and business
continuity. They do not silently replace a business's adopted rules, expand its
mandate or grant cross-business access.

Suspension must name its scope: one business, a capability, or all instances within
the issuer's actual authority. Closing an instance requires preservation or
transfer of that business's obligations. Retiring the master framework must not
silently terminate businesses relying on it.

## Legal documentation boundary

The US/Illinois baseline supplied by the founder is a starting jurisdiction profile
for business schedules, not an Unilogistix incorporation plan. Each actual business
needs its own applicability assessment based on entity/owner, location, customers,
products and data. Entity type, taxes, permits, customer terms and physical-goods
requirements are resolved for that business before the affected activity starts.

Today completes the reusable governing documents and defines these required
business schedules. It does not require inventing an entity for Unilogistix or
choosing the final legal structure of the LondonRue example.
