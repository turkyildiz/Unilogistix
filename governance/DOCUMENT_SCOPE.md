Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft manuscript; detailed adoption and activation pending

## Confirmed direction

The founder has directed that today's work is to complete the company documents,
rules and regulations and agree on their scope. Implementation is outside this
session's current scope. The legal baseline supplied by the founder is United
States / Illinois. These instructions supersede the prior inference that work
should proceed into runtime implementation.

Existing founder directions remain: the founder is the board, AI performs internal
operations, every operational human ask counts as a failure, and suitable existing
infrastructure should be used efficiently. No new budget or production authority
is created by agreement on a document scope.

## Confirmed system and business boundary

Unilogistix will never be an actual company. It is the reusable AI operating
framework. Actual businesses receive dedicated instances that develop and operate
under their own identities. LondonRue selling towels is the founder's example;
this establishes physical-goods coverage alongside software and future authorized
hardware activity. See the [system and business model](SYSTEM_AND_BUSINESS_MODEL.md).

The founder also confirmed the continuous learning loop: outcomes from LondonRue
improve Unilogistix through CI/CD, and the next business uses the improved framework.
Existing instances can receive compatible evaluated upgrades. The documents must
cover business operations and framework improvement as connected responsibilities.

The deliverable has three layers: reusable framework governance; business-instance
rules and specialization schedules; and business-specific legal/operating profiles.
There is no Unilogistix incorporation plan. US/Illinois is the supplied starting
legal profile for actual businesses. Their final owner/entity, municipality,
markets, product facts and activation values are completed in each business schedule.
LondonRue is an example, not a recorded incorporation or launch decision.

## Policy writing standard

Each material rule must identify its purpose, scope, accountable AI function,
trigger, required action, permitted authority, record/evidence, failure response
and exception or escalation route. Include a concrete scenario wherever a boundary
would otherwise be ambiguous. Define deadlines and review cadences as company
proposals unless a verified applicable source establishes them as legal duties.

Keep three categories explicit: founder directions already agreed; company policy
proposals awaiting adoption; and externally imposed requirements supported by
current authoritative sources and applicability facts. Agents cannot amend law,
provider permissions or reserved board authority through policy exceptions.

## Legal and regulatory coverage method

Create an applicability register with requirement, jurisdiction, triggering facts,
official source, verification date, accountable function, deadline rule, evidence,
status and recheck trigger. Record applicable, conditional, not applicable with
reason, and unresolved entries. Do not turn an unreviewed legal topic into a
blanket claim of compliance.

Cover baseline entity administration, records, tax registration and accounting,
contracts and signing authority, truthful marketing, customer remedies, data
protection and incident handling. Evaluate conditional requirements for recurring
billing, commercial email/calls/texts, biometrics, minors, precise location,
health/financial data, regulated services, hardware, accessibility, employment,
external providers, additional states and foreign markets before activating those
activities. Include municipal obligations once the operating location is known.

The AI operating model does not redefine who can legally hold an office, sign a
filing, give a required attestation or perform a regulated professional function.
Document any such requirement accurately and account for its external dependency.
Do not claim that an AI role name satisfies an entity-specific legal requirement.

## Decisions and configuration

The founder's business-instance example resolves the earlier software-only versus
full-business ambiguity. The prior question about incorporating Unilogistix is
superseded. Entity status and customer geography are per-business schedule fields;
they do not prevent completion of the reusable framework manuscript. Final adoption
of the detailed rules and this completion standard remains to be recorded.

Budget amounts, product prices, account identifiers, service targets and portfolio
limits can live in adoption schedules. The governing document must specify who
sets each value, when it is required and what happens if it is absent. Missing
activation values must restrict the affected activity; they must not silently
become unlimited permissions. Sensitive identifiers belong in private records.

## Definition of complete

1. The founder agrees the company boundary, deliverables and exclusions.
2. All required root documents, policies, books and templates are substantively complete; all 307 chapter entries have traceable coverage.
3. Decision rights, autonomy requirements and exception rules agree across the entire collection.
4. Each legal requirement has an official source and applicability assessment; unresolved facts and gated activities are visible.
5. No unresolved question changes the meaning of the agreed core rules. Remaining launch-specific values appear in a bounded activation schedule.
6. Representative scenarios produce an unambiguous owner, decision right, action, record and failure response: routine release, refund, budget exhaustion, security incident, board unavailability, conflicting agents, venture transfer and closure.
7. Navigation, versioning, source citations and cross-references are checked. A document validator confirms structure; substantive scenario review confirms policy coherence.
8. The founder receives one adoption packet identifying the exact version, changes, outstanding activation settings and effect of adoption. Scope agreement and policy adoption are recorded separately.

## Today's exclusions

creation, spending, company filings, customer operations or product launch.
Existing local implementation files remain historical work outside this document
deliverable. Their existence does not establish completion or adoption of any rule.
