Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft; adoption and activation pending

## Authority and reporting

The [constitution](../CONSTITUTION.md#m-reporting-lines-and-independent-oversight) assigns all 60 roles. Actual business executives report to that business’s authorized founder/governance. R07 coordinates independent assurance and R52 independently audits operations **and assurance**, both with protected founder reporting. Unilogistix is a reusable framework, never a legal parent company. Existing permissions and authentic founder directions control; these detailed procedures await adoption.

R04 CFO reports operationally to R01 and has direct protected founder access for financial integrity, exposure and genuine authority issues. That access does not authorize self-payment, raise caps or turn ordinary financial repairs into founder tasks. R54 discrepancies and R40 safety concerns also have protected reporting. Direct visibility means findings cannot be filtered; it does not require a founder click on ordinary operations.

## Reviewer appointment and replacement

Before activating a material gate, R07 records its qualified reviewer pool, conflict rules, protected tests, evidence location, credential issuer, minimum reserved capacity, standby order and assignment algorithm. Actual governance adopts changes to this protected charter; R60 implements reviewed permissions. R01/R03/R04/R55 and other subjects cannot choose a favorable reviewer, replace rejection with a new clean history, withdraw its minimum resources, edit tests or acquire approval keys. R07 cannot approve changes to protections governing an audit of R07.

For each action, inspect authorship, requester, beneficiary, credential custody, execution context and shared host/administrator risks. A CFO-originated purchase requires a different qualified verifier; R48 verification and later reconciliation use separate contexts, with a different principal where required by risk. No identity performs request, verification, payment and reconciliation of its own transaction. Audit has scoped read/report access, never routine payment or deployment authority for the activities it audits.

An unavailable reviewer is replaced by the first eligible qualified standby under the recorded selection rule; preserve all prior verdicts and reasons. A rejecting reviewer is not “unavailable.” No eligible standby means hold the affected action, keep an owned repair task, and continue independent authorized work. Shared unrestricted root credentials invalidate an independence claim even when role names differ.

## Bounded ordinary dispute resolution

As a proposed default, the owner submits one evidence-based appeal within one business day; R07 assigns one conflict-free adjudicator from the approved pool, who decides within one business day of receiving the complete packet. The adjudicator reviews the original finding, full history, policy and new evidence. It can uphold a rejection or correct a factual/application error with reasons, but cannot waive mandatory controls, expand authority or rewrite evidence. Urgent incidents use the incident policy’s shorter containment clocks while the disputed action stays held.

If evidence, qualification or time expires, preserve the restrictive state and create an owned remediation/dependency record with next review date. Reopening requires material new evidence or a corrected artifact, retains the same case history, and never starts reviewer shopping. R52 audits patterns of failed adjudication. Ordinary disputes do not become founder help-desk requests. New authority, genuine constitutional interpretation changing governance, and reserved strategy go to actual governance with a concrete decision packet. Any unavoidable operational human ask is still counted as a failure.

## Stop, recovery and evidence

Enforce authority at the external action boundary using genuinely scoped credentials or exclusive broker custody; broad provider credentials behind a nominal read-only wrapper do not establish isolation. Verify alternative paths, common administrators and emergency privileges explicitly. Provider-enforced checks must bind trusted origin and exact commit/artifact; control/workflow changes take a separate protected path.

R59 observes independently of the worker and founder workstation. R26 owns repair; [watchdog rules](../policies/watchdogs-and-repair-alerts.md) govern delivery, acknowledgment, progress and fallback escalation. Retain evidence off-host under protected access/retention, with lawful deletion and holds. After restore, reapply current revocations, suspensions, deletions and suppression state before serving data or resuming work. A backup is not authority to resurrect canceled work. R52 verifies closure of assurance failures through a different qualified reviewer, and the subject cannot suppress the finding.

The [control register](CONTROL_REGISTER.md) defines the required proof. A drafted rule, successful document push or passing structural validator is not operating control evidence.
