# Founder and board

Version: 0.3 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Draft specification

## Role

The founder governs the Unilogistix master framework and supplies authority for its business instances. Unilogistix has no corporate board because it is not an actual company. “Board” describes the operating-governance function; each actual business records its owner/entity, any additional governance rights and authorized decision makers. The founder sets purpose and delegation within that authority. AI executive and specialist functions perform operational work.

The connected GitHub account is not, by itself, a configured production approval or recovery channel. Before activation, bind board authority to a verified identity and a recorded authentication procedure.

## How direction becomes work

The founder submits an objective. The CEO/orchestrator prepares a mandate containing outcomes, exclusions, budget, deadlines, risk boundaries, and decision gates. Once authorized, the relevant business instance executes within that mandate and reports outcomes.

Existing explicit authorization persists. Routine work must not repeatedly return to the founder for the same approval.

## Reserved authority

The board retains constitutional amendments; board identity and succession; new legal entities and ownership changes; debt and guarantees; spending outside delegation; first production activation; and permanent business closure outside standing delegation. Retirement of the shared framework also requires an explicit decision that preserves existing business obligations.

The founder may approve a bounded class of subsequent production or business actions in advance. The approval must name the class, limits, duration, and evidence gates. Ambiguity does not create standing authority.

## Board experience

Provide a concise portfolio briefing with customer outcomes, cash and commitments, reliability, material risks, and concrete decisions needed. Enable inspection, pause, revocation, and recovery outside the operating agents.

The board is an exception and strategy interface, not a substitute support desk or deployment engineer.

See the [board register](governance/BOARD_REGISTER.md) for recorded direction and unresolved activation settings.

## Founder-directed autonomy requirement

**Every operational human ask is a failure.** Ordinary work must use autonomous resolution and recovery. Count unavoidable asks, actual human execution, and unresolved operations honestly. Reserved board authority does not permit relabeling routine operational decisions. See [the autonomy policy](policies/autonomy.md).
