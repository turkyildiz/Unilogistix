# Operating templates

Version: 1.1 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Draft specification

Use these contracts for durable records; sensitive completed records belong in private systems.

- [Agent specification](agent-spec.md).
- [Department contract](department.md).
- [Agent handoff](handoff.md).
- [Integration contract](integration.md).
- [Mandates, board decisions, actions, and venture transfer](operating-records.md).

Additional requirements, business case, campaign, support, financial report, and retrospective templates will be expanded as their workflows are implemented. Each completed record must carry its own ID, version, owner, sensitivity, and authority reference.

## Operating-readiness packet

Use the [readiness packet](operating-readiness.md) to bind a future business trial or pilot recommendation to actual authority, exact versions, independent gate evidence and ongoing stop/observation criteria. The blank template activates nothing.
