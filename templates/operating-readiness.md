- Applicability, triggering facts and reviewed non-applicability rationale if any:
- Required outcome, responsible delivery owner and independent verifier:

- Receipts, timestamps, cost, retained evidence and unresolved obligations:
- Verdict: pending / failed / passed for exact scope / justified not applicable:

Include the connected lifecycle OR-S01–OR-S10 and applicable GOV tests without double-counting reused evidence as distinct runs. Every applicable launch-critical gate needs independently accepted evidence before pilot recommendation.
