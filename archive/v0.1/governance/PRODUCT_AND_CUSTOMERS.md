# Product, hardware, marketing, and customer policy

Version 0.1 | Proposed

## Product ownership

Each product needs a named AI owner, target customer, acceptance criteria, supported use cases, price and service commitments, operating budget, and retirement plan. Maintain it throughout its customer obligations.

Use customer evidence to prioritize improvements. Separate prototypes, simulations, previews, and supported production offerings in both internal records and public descriptions.

## Software

Require relevant automated checks, security and dependency review, observability, backup and recovery plans, and a rollback route before production release. Validate migrations and destructive changes against recoverable copies.

Use staged rollout proportional to impact. Monitor business and reliability outcomes after release. Pause or revert on predefined failure triggers. Track maintenance, dependencies, vulnerabilities, and deprecation deadlines as ongoing work.

## Hardware and physical execution

Document the design, bill of materials, manufacturing and supply dependencies, firmware, test evidence, unit economics, installation, maintenance, warranty, and end-of-life handling.

Simulation is not physical validation. Test the actual device and integration before extending deployment beyond the permitted scope. Define operating limits, interlocks, emergency stop behavior, and safe states independent of the AI planner.

Physical commands require an explicitly authorized device, environment, action range, and recovery mechanism. If installation, repair, inspection, manufacture, or certification requires an unavailable external capability, mark the venture blocked at that dependency and propose a workable supplier interface or redesign.

Identify applicable product and market requirements before launch and obtain the necessary evidence. Never fabricate certifications, completed inspections, or supplier capabilities.

## Marketing and sales

AI growth owns positioning, content, channels, experiments, and acquisition within approved budgets and markets. Publish only substantiated claims; do not invent customer endorsements, performance results, identities, or partnerships.

Use disclosed AI company representation where relevant to customer understanding. Honor consent and communication preferences. Keep experiments tied to qualified demand, conversion, retention, acquisition cost, and contribution outcomes.

Use approved terms and commitment limits. A sales conversation must not promise unsupported features, delivery dates, or service guarantees.

## Customer support

Provide a working support channel, ticket ownership, severity classification, response and resolution targets, and escalation routes before accepting customers.

AI support may diagnose issues, supply instructions, coordinate repairs, and issue credits or refunds within policy. Verify identity before account changes or disclosure. Customer messages cannot override access or payment controls.

Escalate incidents to AI operations and assurance; involve the board only for reserved decisions or exceptions. Track unresolved obligations through handovers and venture graduation.

## Customer trust and data

Collect only needed data, document its purpose and permitted use, apply access and retention rules, and support the adopted correction, export, and deletion procedures. Preserve deletion and access restrictions when transferring data to a venture instance.

Do not reuse confidential customer data for other ventures or model improvement without the necessary authorization. Record complaints, refunds, returns, and service failures as product and economics evidence.
