# Unilogistix

**An AI-operated company that turns board ideas into enduring businesses.**

The founder is the board. Unilogistix performs the work: research, software and hardware development, operations, finance administration, marketing, customer support, continuous improvement, and venture creation. Its target operating model has no human employees or routine human operators.

When a venture proves viable, Unilogistix instantiates a dedicated AI operating company for it, transfers the necessary systems and responsibilities, retains portfolio oversight, and takes on the next project.

## Status

Founding governance proposal, version 0.1 — 2026-09-09.

This repository establishes the intended operating model. It does not yet contain a running autonomous company. Publication records the proposal; it does not record board ratification, authorize spending, establish a legal entity, or provision operational controls.

The founder's stated vision is recorded in the charter. Detailed policy choices are proposed defaults pending an explicit board decision. Work on documentation, research, and reversible local prototypes can continue under the founder's existing instructions.

## First activation

The board ratifies a specific governance commit, establishes its authenticated decision channel, and issues the first venture mandate with measurable objectives, a budget, and an authority envelope. The operating system must then implement and verify the required controls before receiving production credentials.

Thereafter, routine execution belongs to the AI company. The board directs strategy and handles reserved decisions and exceptions.
