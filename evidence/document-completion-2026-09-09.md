# Ten-book document completion evidence

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Documentary review; runtime acceptance tests not run

## Deliverable and method

The [library](../books/README.md) expands all 307 original blueprint chapter entries into ten manuscripts. Each chapter has owner/trigger, rules/procedure and acceptance/failure content. The constitution supplies 60 canonical six-field role contracts; the books inherit shared authority and control rules. The coverage matrix links every chapter to its exact anchor.

Validation checks the original blueprint lists, chapter titles/counts, required fields, role IDs, document metadata and relative links. Documentary scenario review below checks how written rules resolve representative cases. This is author review, not a claim of independent audit, deployed enforcement, legal certification or a live simulation. The 18 adversarial cases in the [control register](../governance/CONTROL_REGISTER.md) are specified and unrun.

## Scenario review of written rules

| Case | Owner and decision right | Action and record | Failure disposition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Routine release | R03 delivery; R20/R21/R23 independent checks; R25 executor within mandate | Exact artifact, trusted results, acceptance and rollback receipts | Failed mandatory check blocks release; R26 owns repair/containment |
| LondonRue refund / uncertain payment | R35/R41 customer remedy; independent R48 verification; R47 execution | Supported identity/order, policy limit, action key and source settlement | Reconcile original result before retry; R35 retains customer obligation |
| Budget exhausted / concurrent spend | R04 envelope; R43 allocation; R47 boundary | Atomic reservation and total exposure, including uncertain external results | No over-cap action; authorized alternative or owned blocked task, no self-raised cap |
| Security incident | One R26 commander; R23/R51 specialist checks; R59 observer | Preauthorized containment, impact, alert receipts and repair evidence | Repeat/fallback alerts and independent closure; compromised workers cannot silence observer |
| Founder unavailable | R07 authentic existing mandate; R60 stop enforcement | Continue only existing delegated activity; independent recovery record | New reserved authority stays pending; ordinary problems follow autonomous recovery |
| Conflicting agents | R07 assigns conflict-free adjudicator under protected pool | One appeal, full prior evidence, reasoned disposition and deadline | Restrictive timeout; no reviewer shopping or ordinary founder help desk |
| Business transfer / new instance | R58 coordination under actual mandate; operating owner accepts obligations | Isolated identity, scoped assets/data, financial/customer handover and rollback | Unowned obligations block handover; learning alone never transfers credentials or power |
| Closure / restored old state | R58/R02 continuity; R44/R48 reconciliation; R51/R60 current restrictions | Notices/remedies, retained lawful records, deletion/stop reconciliation | No abandoned customer debt or resurrection of revoked authority after restore |

All cases have a written owner, authority boundary, action/record and failure route. Numeric clocks, budgets, actual legal facts and production evidence remain in the bounded activation schedule. An unresolved setting restricts its action rather than silently expanding permission.
