# Safe Goes — candidate product

Version: 0.2 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Draft specification

## Purpose and status

A proposed GPS-tracking business and an early test of UNI's complete operating model. No live location collection, purchase, or launch is authorized by this brief.

## Candidate capability map

Device provisioning and management; vehicle and live-location tracking; historical location; alerts and geofencing; account and customer dashboards; notifications; billing; support; analytics; administration; marketing; sales; and onboarding.

These are research candidates, not a committed scope. Validate customers, device/protocol feasibility, differentiators, costs, and demand before fixing requirements.

## Discovery deliverables

Customer problem brief, evidence-based market alternatives, SWOT, business case, device/supplier dependencies, data-flow and access model, unit economics, service expectations, and a bounded initial product mandate.

Use simulated location and test identities during initial development. Specify who is authorized to enroll and track devices, who can view history, retention, revocation, and misuse handling before real data is collected.

## First acceptance proposal

An authorized test device or simulator sends location to an isolated test account; only that account can view it; a geofence event produces an auditable notification; device revocation stops access; retention is demonstrable.

Later business acceptance includes onboarding, billing reconciliation, support, reliability, and zero operational human asks across the adopted observation window.
