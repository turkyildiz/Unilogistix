# Freightex first-project preparation

Version: 0.1 | Updated: 2026-09-10 | Status: Business model and first customer designated; detailed delivery scope proposed; pre-launch

The founder clarified that Freightex is a platform for brokers, has not launched,
and has no customers yet. He designated his own brokerage as its first customer
and proposed Freightex as Unilogistix's first project to develop through launch.
The customer identity, environment mapping and detailed pilot charter remain in
private project records. See F-022/F-023 in the [board register](../../governance/BOARD_REGISTER.md).

Unilogistix is the reusable operating framework; Freightex is the platform
business. The pilot brokerage is a customer organization. Platform finances,
customer brokerage books, customer data and credentials must remain distinct.
Existing hosted records do not establish paying platform customers or their
provenance. Preserve them while qualifying an isolated synthetic baseline.

Maestro coordinates product scope, engineering, independent review, finance/data
readiness and operations. Preparation covers the first customer workflow,
two-tenant isolation, exact-source tests and releases, data provenance,
accounting controls, recovery, support, incident reporting and a reviewable
launch packet. Validated generic lessons feed back into the framework; customer
records and credentials do not.

The detailed scope is a proposal for agreement. Existing authorized repair work
continues. No launch date, pricing, additional spending, historical financial
rewrite, provider activation or public customer announcement is inferred from
the designation. Follow the [business-instance model](../../governance/SYSTEM_AND_BUSINESS_MODEL.md)
and [instance adoption schedule](../../templates/business-instance.md).
