# Product portfolio

Version: 0.3 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Draft specification

[Safe Goes](safe-goes/README.md) is the master blueprint's proposed first product. It is not the parent UNI system and is not yet an authorized live venture.

[LondonRue](../governance/SYSTEM_AND_BUSINESS_MODEL.md) is the founder's example towel business: its dedicated instance develops and operates as LondonRue. It is not recorded as formed or launched. Unilogistix is the reusable framework, not a portfolio holding company.

Every product gets its own mandate, accountable AI owner, financial boundary, data boundary, service commitments, and lifecycle evidence. Graduation follows [the venture policy](../policies/venture-lifecycle.md).
