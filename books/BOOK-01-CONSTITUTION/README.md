# Company Constitution & Governance

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft chapter manuscript; adoption and runtime activation pending

## Operating contract

The founder governs purpose and reserved decisions. The executive layer manages delegated work. Every product and agent is bound to an effective policy version and a mandate; adoption and tool authorization must be independently verifiable.

[CONSTITUTION.md](../../CONSTITUTION.md) is the canonical constitutional text. [RULEBOOK.md](../../RULEBOOK.md) contains mandatory agent requirements. Policy details live in [policies](../../policies/README.md).

## Decision procedure

The [constitutional agent responsibility schedule](../../CONSTITUTION.md#constitutional-agent-responsibility-schedule)
contains the completed draft role contracts, common obligations, separation rules
and cross-department scenarios. This book references that single canonical schedule;
it does not grant an alternative set of permissions.

Classify the proposed action as automatic, agent-reviewed, executive-reviewed, or founder-approved. Identify all applicable financial, security, physical, customer, and release gates. Verify that both authority and acceptance evidence exist before execution.

Founder approval may be supplied by an explicit, unexpired standing mandate covering the action. No reviewer can increase that mandate. Governance amendment and changes of board identity remain founder decisions.

## Accountability and exceptions

Record requester, accountable owner, reviewer, mandate, evidence, outcome, and effective policy version. Resolve disagreements through the responsible executive; unresolved authority disputes go to the founder. An exception must identify its scope, expiry, compensating controls, and original approval.

Immediate containment is available under predefined incident authority. Restoration uses the applicable incident or board gate. Retire an agent by stopping queued work, transferring obligations, revoking credentials, and preserving its records.

## Review and evidence

Review governance after material incidents and on the adopted cadence. Before activation demonstrate denial of a forged approval, expired mandate, self-approved action, over-limit payment, and revoked agent. Demonstrate that legitimate independent work continues when one decision is blocked.

## Outstanding decisions

The [board register](../../governance/BOARD_REGISTER.md) holds adoption status, identity setup, financial limits, and production activation requirements. This book does not infer legal entity arrangements or assign an operational budget.

## Autonomous operation requirement

Every operational human ask is a failure. Apply [the founder-directed autonomy policy](../../policies/autonomy.md), with AI recovery, honest failure accounting, and no authority bypass. Routine escalations go to AI functions; reserved board decisions remain explicit.
