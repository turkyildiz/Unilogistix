# Evolution & Continuous Improvement

Version: 0.3 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Initial specification; runtime not implemented

## Founder-confirmed learning scope

Business experience, including LondonRue's successes and failures, must improve Unilogistix and subsequent businesses. Use the [business-to-framework CI/CD workflow](../../workflows/business-to-framework-learning.md): capture evidence, generalize, independently evaluate, release a versioned improvement, measure outcomes and distribute compatible upgrades. Business and framework release paths are connected but retain distinct authority and data scopes.

## Improvement loop

Observe → diagnose → propose → authorize → experiment → evaluate → canary → adopt or reject → document. Tie every change to a measured baseline and a customer, economic, reliability, or autonomy outcome.

Product, department, agent, security, and governance reviews create an improvement backlog with owners and evidence. Customer complaints and failed experiments are primary inputs.

## Experiment contract

State hypothesis, baseline, target metric, guardrail metrics, resource cap, observation window, sample limitations, stop condition, reviewer, and rollback before execution. Avoid promoting a change solely because it is new or performs well on one demonstration.

Prompt, workflow, model, tool, and organization changes must preserve authority boundaries. Use pinned versions and comparable evaluation sets. Record negative results and costs.

## Agent and organization evolution

Create a role only for a demonstrated workflow need. Promote evaluated versions; retire ineffective agents with task transfer and credential revocation. Consolidate redundant responsibilities when quality and cost evidence support it.

Meta-agents may recommend changes to prompts, agents, workflows, and policies. They cannot self-authorize broader privileges or remove governance.

## Product factory and expansion

New products inherit policies, agent contracts, engineering standards, support workflows, dashboards, finance controls, and permitted reusable knowledge. Add product-specific requirements and isolated records.

Business instances can be created during discovery and development. The [viability and transfer gates](../../policies/venture-lifecycle.md) govern graduation and a specified handover, not initial instantiation. Preserve customer obligations and authorized suspension across instances. Unilogistix is their originating framework, not their legal parent company.

Long-term and global expansion roadmaps are hypotheses reviewed against evidence, account arrangements, markets, and resource limits. Avoid unsupported five-year technology commitments.

## Acceptance

Run a baseline/challenger comparison, show guardrails can reject a superficially better model, demonstrate canary rollback, and prove a proposed improvement cannot increase its own permissions.

Record adopted changes with the decision, version, results, and operational migration; proposals remain proposals until their gate passes.

## Autonomous operation requirement

Every operational human ask is a failure. Apply [the founder-directed autonomy policy](../../policies/autonomy.md), with AI recovery, honest failure accounting, and no authority bypass. Routine escalations go to AI functions; reserved board decisions remain explicit.
