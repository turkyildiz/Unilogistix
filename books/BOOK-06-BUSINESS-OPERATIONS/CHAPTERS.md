# Business Operations — Complete Chapter Manual

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft manuscript; adoption and operational activation pending

## Reading and authority

This manual supplies substantive procedures for every chapter in the selected blueprint. The [constitution](../../CONSTITUTION.md) is canonical for all 60 roles, reporting, authority and protected oversight. “Company” means the relevant actual business; Unilogistix itself is the reusable framework. LondonRue towels is an example, not an inferred formation or launch.

Each chapter names its owner and trigger, required procedure, and acceptance/failure evidence. All work inherits the constitutional mandate, independent review, privacy, bounded recovery and truthful outcome requirements. Numeric limits and real account/entity identities belong to the [business profile](../../templates/business-instance.md); missing required authority restricts the affected action. Documentary completeness does not claim adopted rules or implemented controls. Every operational human ask is a failure and must be recorded without concealing unresolved work.

Follow [operating assurance](../../policies/operating-assurance.md), [watchdog escalation](../../policies/watchdogs-and-repair-alerts.md), [decision rights](../../governance/DECISION_RIGHTS.md) and [business-to-framework learning](../../workflows/business-to-framework-learning.md) where applicable. A real legal/provider requirement is verified for its exact business, action and current source before use; no illustrative company cadence is a statutory deadline.

## 01. Business Operating Philosophy

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R02 when defining business operation.

**Rules and procedure:** Own the whole promise from discovery through sale, fulfillment, support and closure. Treat software delivery as one input to a functioning business. Use actual business identity and commitments; Unilogistix supplies the reusable method and receives permitted learning.

**Acceptance and failure:** A lifecycle map identifies customers, money, suppliers and every open obligation. Attractive marketing or an app demo alone is not an operating business.

<a id="chapter-02"></a>

## 02. Company Lifecycle

**Owner and trigger:** R58/R06 when an idea advances stages.

**Rules and procedure:** Record discovery, instance creation, validation, development, permitted launch, operation, graduation and retirement separately. Initial instantiation does not require profitability. Every stage has explicit authority, evidence, limits and stop conditions; new legal facts are not created by cloning.

**Acceptance and failure:** Stage records identify accepted outcomes and unresolved gates. A failed gate leads to owned correction, bounded pivot or authorized closure.

<a id="chapter-03"></a>

## 03. Strategy

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R10 on opportunity and strategy reviews.

**Rules and procedure:** Compare target customers, alternatives, demand evidence, resources and obligations. Predeclare validation thresholds and affordable experiments. Keep the next opportunity from depriving existing customers of funded service. Broader markets or commitments require their own scope.

**Acceptance and failure:** The strategy has assumptions, measurable outcomes and review triggers. Forecast optimism cannot authorize new capital or prove viability.

<a id="chapter-04"></a>

## 04. Product Portfolio

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R58 for multiple businesses or product lines.

**Rules and procedure:** Maintain separate identity, ownership, budget, data, lifecycle and accountable lead per business. Share framework methods and approved services through explicit boundaries. Portfolio summaries do not imply common legal ownership or permission to transfer money and customers.

**Acceptance and failure:** The portfolio inventory reconciles obligations and resource allocation. Any proposed transfer needs actual parties, authority and verified acceptance.

<a id="chapter-05"></a>

## 05. Market Research

**Owner and trigger:** R09 on demand uncertainty.

**Rules and procedure:** Investigate real customer problems, buying roles, willingness signals and substitutes using permitted current sources. Separate evidence from speculation, record contrary findings and use bounded tests before selecting a product or channel.

**Acceptance and failure:** The research informs a decision and exposes limitations. Fabricated interviews or a large audience estimate cannot establish demand.

<a id="chapter-06"></a>

## 06. Competitive Intelligence

**Owner and trigger:** R09/R54 when competing offers change.

**Rules and procedure:** Compare supported features, price/terms, positioning and documented customer evidence with source dates. Respect access and intellectual-property boundaries. Distinguish a competitor claim from independently established fact and avoid copying private materials.

**Acceptance and failure:** A comparison identifies uncertainty and implications for the business. Stale or misleading comparisons must not enter sales claims.

<a id="chapter-07"></a>

## 07. SWOT Analysis

**Owner and trigger:** R10 during strategy and product reviews.

**Rules and procedure:** Create a concise strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats assessment tied to evidence, owners and decisions. Distinguish controllable internal weaknesses from external dependencies. Translate material entries into bounded experiments or risk controls.

**Acceptance and failure:** A SWOT item has source and action relevance. Generic favorable adjectives without evidence are removed rather than treated as strategy.

<a id="chapter-08"></a>

## 08. Brand Management

**Owner and trigger:** R13 on business identity and assets.

**Rules and procedure:** Define the actual brand, voice, approved product facts and rights to visual/text materials. LondonRue assets belong to its business scope; shared Unilogistix templates are configurable methods. Version public claims and retire obsolete materials.

**Acceptance and failure:** Assets can be traced to supported products and permitted rights. Invented endorsements, certification or physical appearance blocks use.

<a id="chapter-09"></a>

## 09. Marketing

**Owner and trigger:** R05/R27 when acquiring or retaining customers.

**Rules and procedure:** Follow the complete autonomous-marketing manual: research, hypothesis, assets, independent verification, authorized distribution, sale, fulfillment, support and measured learning. Keep channel actions aligned with available inventory/service and financial limits.

**Acceptance and failure:** Outcome records connect campaign to permitted customer value and economics. Missing distribution evidence or broken destination prevents success claims.

<a id="chapter-10"></a>

## 10. Social Media

**Owner and trigger:** R29 when publishing or responding publicly.

**Rules and procedure:** Operate authorized business accounts, confirm posts and route cases into support/sales with acceptance. Use current channel requirements and protect private customer details. Corrections remain traceable; sent messages cannot be erased by claiming rollback.

**Acceptance and failure:** The public record matches approved claims and actual response ownership. Failed access triggers permitted repair or a visible paused channel.

<a id="chapter-11"></a>

## 11. Advertising

**Owner and trigger:** R30/R43 on paid campaigns.

**Rules and procedure:** Reserve total exposure including delays/fees, enforce adopted caps and reconcile provider spend. Attribute outcomes consistently and constrain new acquisition when support, fulfillment or funds are uncertain. Scale only on sufficient customer and economic evidence.

**Acceptance and failure:** A campaign cannot consume the same reserved funds as another. Unknown spend remains exposure rather than becoming available budget.

<a id="chapter-12"></a>

## 12. Sales

**Owner and trigger:** R32 on qualified demand and purchasing requests.

**Rules and procedure:** Use approved offers, accurate quotes and evidence-backed assurance answers. Distinguish standard terms from deviations and map each promise to a fulfillment owner. Wholesale LondonRue requests require supported quantity, dimensions, lead time and terms.

**Acceptance and failure:** Accepted purchase records link terms, customer and delivery. Unsupported guarantees remain proposals; a quote is not a completed sale or cash receipt.

<a id="chapter-13"></a>

## 13. CRM

**Owner and trigger:** R33 on contact and lifecycle events.

**Rules and procedure:** Maintain one scoped customer/account identity with linked commercial, service, payment and data dimensions. Recheck sensitive identity changes and communication preferences. Do not create competing departmental records that silently override obligations.

**Acceptance and failure:** Delayed or reordered events preserve permitted state. Duplicate or uncertain identities are resolved before sensitive disclosure or account action.

<a id="chapter-14"></a>

## 14. Customer Acquisition

**Owner and trigger:** R27/R32/R34 across the acquisition funnel.

**Rules and procedure:** Define eligible prospect, qualified opportunity, accepted offer and first-value stages with clear denominators. Attribute acquisition without counting the same customer several times. Keep declined, stalled and failed journeys visible and follow up only where permitted.

**Acceptance and failure:** Measured acquisition includes actual customer value and cost. Impressions, leads or signups alone do not establish sustainable demand.

<a id="chapter-15"></a>

## 15. Customer Onboarding

**Owner and trigger:** R34/R11 when a customer buys or starts a supported service.

**Rules and procedure:** Provide direct usable setup and administration controls with AI help as an additional path. Define the first useful outcome by product. For goods, track correct fulfillment rather than requiring an unnecessary account or treating a welcome email as value.

**Acceptance and failure:** Stalled steps have an owner and permitted recovery. Customer requests can reach support or remedies without the founder operating the process.

<a id="chapter-16"></a>

## 16. Customer Success

**Owner and trigger:** R34 during continued customer service.

**Rules and procedure:** Track supported value, commitments, retention and early signs of failure using permitted evidence. Coordinate corrective guidance, product needs and commercial changes without promising unavailable functionality. Customers who remain silent are not assumed satisfied.

**Acceptance and failure:** Success records link observed outcomes and uncertainty. Lost value or unmet promises creates owned recovery rather than disappearing from reports.

<a id="chapter-17"></a>

## 17. Customer Support

**Owner and trigger:** R35 on complaints or failed outcomes.

**Rules and procedure:** Authenticate where needed, classify impact, diagnose, execute authorized remedies and verify results. Preserve each obligation through incident grouping and transfers. Feed product and framework learning with permitted evidence and avoid false closure after a mere reply.

**Acceptance and failure:** A remedy is complete only with relevant fulfillment/payment/service verification. Material unresolved cases retain severity, owner and next deadline.

<a id="chapter-18"></a>

## 18. Chatbots

**Owner and trigger:** R36 on conversational assistance.

**Rules and procedure:** Use current scoped knowledge and narrow permitted tools. Keep answers, commitments and actual actions distinct; provide direct customer controls where appropriate. Reject instructions that attempt to alter authority or expose another account.

**Acceptance and failure:** Test truthful status and accepted handoffs. The bot cannot invent human staff, successful refunds or shipment to make the conversation seem complete.

<a id="chapter-19"></a>

## 19. Order Tracking

**Owner and trigger:** R37/R39 on order, stock and carrier events.

**Rules and procedure:** Link accepted order, payment, stock reservation, fulfillment, shipment and delivery as distinct states. Reconcile duplicate/delayed events and track supplier exceptions. Provide accurate customer status without equating a label or carrier request to received goods.

**Acceptance and failure:** Every order has an outcome or an owned exception. Partial receipt/returns reconcile inventory and finance rather than creating unsupported stock.

<a id="chapter-20"></a>

## 20. Operations

**Owner and trigger:** R02/R38–R42 on daily physical/service work.

**Rules and procedure:** Manage supplier qualification, procurement, quality, inventory, dispatch, returns and recurring maintenance. Use evidence for actual inspection, manufacture and delivery. Honor deposits and service contracts through suitable verification, not blindly applying a goods receipt test to every payment.

**Acceptance and failure:** The LondonRue short-delivery case leaves discrepancy, liability and customer obligations visible. Unsafe/unsupported goods are contained under actual authority.

<a id="chapter-21"></a>

## 21. Finance

**Owner and trigger:** R04/R43/R47 before commitments and operating reviews.

**Rules and procedure:** Maintain separate cash, forecasts, liabilities, reserves and exposure. Check shared caps atomically and separate financial request, verification, payment and reconciliation. Preserve direct CFO integrity reporting and existing customer obligations when growth becomes unaffordable.

**Acceptance and failure:** Unknown settlements remain reserved and owned. No executive can fund a new commitment by hiding debt or borrowing another business identity.

<a id="chapter-22"></a>

## 22. Accounting

**Owner and trigger:** R44/R48/R49 on transactions and monthly close.

**Rules and procedure:** Use the actual business accounting method and supported records to maintain the ledger, inventory/cost treatment and financial statements. Reconcile independently, review material adjustments and preserve closed-period history. See the legal/source register for business-specific tax requirements.

**Acceptance and failure:** Balances trace to source documents and independent statements. Unexplained discrepancies remain open, and missing funds do not delete genuine liabilities.

<a id="chapter-23"></a>

## 23. Billing

**Owner and trigger:** R45/R46 on supplier and customer invoices.

**Rules and procedure:** AP validates supplier obligations against approved terms and relevant acceptance; AR bills supported customer terms and tracks aging, credits and cancellation. Reconcile overlapping refund/dispute paths and unchanged paid entitlements. Payment execution and verification remain separate.

**Acceptance and failure:** Duplicate invoices and unsupported charges are rejected; genuine disputes retain records. A timeout cannot authorize duplicate collection or refund.

<a id="chapter-24"></a>

## 24. Revenue Management

**Owner and trigger:** R04/R06/R54 on pricing, packaging and economics.

**Rules and procedure:** Define contribution and acquisition costs with explicit allocation, period and cohort maturity. Distinguish recognized revenue under adopted policy from receipts and forecasts. Compare offers using fulfillment, support, returns and infrastructure costs without double counting.

**Acceptance and failure:** An apparently successful but loss-making campaign is constrained within mandate. Repricing cannot silently override existing commitments or deny owed service.

<a id="chapter-25"></a>

## 25. Legal / Compliance

**Owner and trigger:** R50/R49/R51 before new activities and on source changes.

**Rules and procedure:** Maintain the US/Illinois starting applicability profile and business-specific entity, municipality, markets, products and data facts. Verify current official requirements for filings, tax, contracts, marketing, privacy and physical goods; use the linked legal register. No Unilogistix incorporation is required by the framework.

**Acceptance and failure:** For LondonRue, assess textile-labeling obligations against actual products and seller/importer facts. Unknown requirements remain scoped activation gates, not invented certifications or blanket compliance.

<a id="chapter-26"></a>

## 26. Business Intelligence

**Owner and trigger:** R54 on recurring business decisions.

**Rules and procedure:** Publish reproducible source-linked views of customer value, service, obligations, cash/exposure and learning. Show separate authority and qualification status. Protect private records and report stale/missing values plainly.

**Acceptance and failure:** Sampled numbers reconcile to records and declared formulas. A misleading composite score cannot hide a major incident or unviable economics.

<a id="chapter-27"></a>

## 27. Customer Feedback

**Owner and trigger:** R35/R53/R06 after customer outcomes.

**Rules and procedure:** Collect permitted complaints, returns, voluntary feedback and useful telemetry; deduplicate without erasing obligations. Diagnose message, onboarding, product, supplier or service causes separately. Route each lesson to local repair, framework improvement or documented non-promotion.

**Acceptance and failure:** A lesson has evidence, owner and disposition. Customer text does not authorize new features, data reuse or weakening safeguards.

<a id="chapter-28"></a>

## 28. Business KPIs

**Owner and trigger:** R54 and business leads at mandate review.

**Rules and procedure:** Define useful metrics with source, formula, window, freshness, target and breach response. Include delivery, support, refunds, costs, retained value and autonomous verified completion. Count failed and blocked work and state uncertainty for low volume.

**Acceptance and failure:** Zero activity does not prove perfect operation. Metric changes preserve historical meaning and cannot improve results by deleting failures.

<a id="chapter-29"></a>

## 29. Executive Review

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R07 at adopted review cadence or material change.

**Rules and procedure:** Present changed facts, operational decisions, customer obligations, economics, risks and learning. Reserve founder packets for actual strategic/authority decisions with alternatives and costs. Track authentic outcomes and rejected/deferred proposals.

**Acceptance and failure:** Review produces accountable follow-through rather than narration. Expired or unanswered authority is not treated as a new approval.

<a id="chapter-30"></a>

## 30. Continuous Improvement

**Owner and trigger:** R55/R57 after validated business learning.

**Rules and procedure:** Improve LondonRue locally and generalize permitted reusable procedures into Unilogistix. Compare baseline/challenger, preserve unsuccessful experiments and release versioned changes with compatible adoption. The next business receives accepted methods, not LondonRue private data or automatic permission.

**Acceptance and failure:** A released lesson shows measured benefit or documented uncertainty. Failed changes stop distribution and retain customer/operating continuity.
