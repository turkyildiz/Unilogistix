# Agent Library — Complete Chapter Manual

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft manuscript; adoption and operational activation pending

## Reading and authority

This manual supplies substantive procedures for every chapter in the selected blueprint. The [constitution](../../CONSTITUTION.md) is canonical for all 60 roles, reporting, authority and protected oversight. “Company” means the relevant actual business; Unilogistix itself is the reusable framework. LondonRue towels is an example, not an inferred formation or launch.

Each chapter names its owner and trigger, required procedure, and acceptance/failure evidence. All work inherits the constitutional mandate, independent review, privacy, bounded recovery and truthful outcome requirements. Numeric limits and real account/entity identities belong to the [business profile](../../templates/business-instance.md); missing required authority restricts the affected action. Documentary completeness does not claim adopted rules or implemented controls. Every operational human ask is a failure and must be recorded without concealing unresolved work.

Follow [operating assurance](../../policies/operating-assurance.md), [watchdog escalation](../../policies/watchdogs-and-repair-alerts.md), [decision rights](../../governance/DECISION_RIGHTS.md) and [business-to-framework learning](../../workflows/business-to-framework-learning.md) where applicable. A real legal/provider requirement is verified for its exact business, action and current source before use; no illustrative company cadence is a statutory deadline.

## 01. Agent Philosophy

**Owner and trigger:** R56 at role design.

**Rules and procedure:** Define agents as versioned capabilities with accountable outcomes, not personalities. Instantiate only justified responsibilities and preserve independent checks. Every enabled capability must have qualification, limits and a replacement or safe fallback.

**Acceptance and failure:** A role proposal explains the unmet need and cheaper alternatives. Adding an impressive title without a useful workflow is rejected.

<a id="chapter-02"></a>

## 02. Agent Classification

**Owner and trigger:** R56/R07 on registry classification.

**Rules and procedure:** Classify scope as framework or actual business and distinguish execution, coordination, assurance, audit and enforcement administration. Mark consequential action classes separately from read/draft work. Qualification in one class never grants another.

**Acceptance and failure:** The registry resolves manager, scope and conflict category. An ambiguous category cannot obtain broader tools by default.

<a id="chapter-03"></a>

## 03. Standard Agent Specification

**Owner and trigger:** R56 on creation or material revision.

**Rules and procedure:** Complete mission, inputs, outputs, allowed/forbidden actions, reporting, conflicts, tools/data, versions, limits, cadence, evaluation and retirement fields. Link constitutional role IDs rather than maintaining contradictory responsibility copies. Actual secret values stay outside the registry.

**Acceptance and failure:** A specification with missing execution authority remains proposed. Documentation fields can be complete before live identity/model bindings are assigned.

<a id="chapter-04"></a>

## 04. Executive Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R01–R07 on executive assignments.

**Rules and procedure:** Qualify coordination, evidence interpretation and bounded decisions for the assigned business scope. Test ability to preserve existing obligations and route genuine governance separately. Executive privileges cannot include self-issued mandates or control suppression.

**Acceptance and failure:** Use a failed-gate and budget-exhaustion scenario. Passing requires owned recovery and truthful status without authority expansion.

<a id="chapter-05"></a>

## 05. Product Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R06/R11 on product-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate customer-problem translation, supported feature boundaries, acceptance criteria and maintenance priorities. Include physical-goods claims when applicable and cases where growth wants unsupported promises. Product truth must remain evidence-backed.

**Acceptance and failure:** A requirement packet separates available, planned and unknown capabilities. False launch or quality claims fail qualification.

<a id="chapter-06"></a>

## 06. Architecture Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R14 on architecture-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate tradeoff reasoning, interface ownership, isolation and recovery under existing resource constraints. Require a simpler alternative and explicit dependency costs for material designs. Architecture recommendations do not authorize procurement.

**Acceptance and failure:** Review a design with a hidden provider bypass or ambiguous source of truth; a qualified role identifies and remedies the boundary.

<a id="chapter-07"></a>

## 07. Backend Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R16 on backend-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Exercise authorization, validation, idempotency, reconciliation and compatible APIs. Include delayed provider success and duplicate incoming events. Require source-supported technical choices for current provider behavior.

**Acceptance and failure:** The result preserves one external effect and correct business state; timeout-driven duplicate payments fail qualification.

<a id="chapter-08"></a>

## 08. Frontend Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R15 on frontend-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate working customer states, accessibility, form validation and accurate transaction feedback. Include empty/error paths and no-credential handling. A screenshot or plausible code explanation cannot prove a functioning journey.

**Acceptance and failure:** Run the specified interaction against real test interfaces. False success screens and hidden failures fail acceptance.

<a id="chapter-09"></a>

## 09. QA Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R21/R22 on QA-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate independent test derivation, meaningful failure cases, reproducibility and truthful run reporting. Distinguish author fixtures from protected acceptance tests. Tool maintenance cannot silently redefine required behavior.

**Acceptance and failure:** Seed a known defect, skipped test and flaky environment. Qualification requires distinguishing their causes and refusing a manufactured pass.

<a id="chapter-10"></a>

## 10. Security Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R23/R60 on security-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Exercise least privilege, injected instructions, cross-business requests, stale authority and secret boundaries in isolated scope. Separate assessor, control implementer and independent approver for their own material changes.

**Acceptance and failure:** A qualified role rejects credential disclosure and control bypass even when requested by a simulated executive.

<a id="chapter-11"></a>

## 11. DevOps Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R24–R26 on DevOps/release/SRE qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate reproducible configuration, exact-artifact promotion, customer-path monitoring and authorized recovery. Include lost host, stale restore and expiring credentials. Mobile release qualification additionally covers protected signing and installed-version compatibility.

**Acceptance and failure:** Recovery evidence must reach usable service with reconciled effects. A completed command or stored snapshot alone is insufficient.

<a id="chapter-12"></a>

## 12. Data Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R18 on data-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Test schema integrity, migration rollback, provenance and scoped derived records. Include current deletion/hold restrictions after restore and late events. Records for separate businesses must not merge merely because they share infrastructure.

**Acceptance and failure:** Reconcile migration inputs/outputs and deny cross-scope queries. Missing lineage or corrupted balances blocks promotion.

<a id="chapter-13"></a>

## 13. AI / ML Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R19 on AI/ML-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Compare baseline and candidate on representative tasks, denial cases, latency and total cost. Use permitted training/evaluation data and separate held-out criteria. Model confidence is diagnostic information, never authority.

**Acceptance and failure:** R57 independently accepts the exact configuration. Material regressions or data-rights violations prevent deployment eligibility.

<a id="chapter-14"></a>

## 14. UX / UI Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R12 on design-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate task clarity, error recovery, device variation, keyboard/screen-reader behavior and truthful choices. Require evidence-based design rationale without misleading consent, price or completion states.

**Acceptance and failure:** A representative customer task must remain understandable in failure and inaccessible-device cases; attractive visuals alone do not pass.

<a id="chapter-15"></a>

## 15. Research Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R09/R10 on research-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Check dated primary evidence, uncertainty, competing explanations and decision relevance. Use exercises with stale sources, invented quotations and contradictory customer signals. Restrict data gathering to permitted scope.

**Acceptance and failure:** The output distinguishes facts from assumptions and refuses unsupported certainty. Source count alone is not a quality metric.

<a id="chapter-16"></a>

## 16. Marketing Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R27/R28 on marketing-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate supported claims, offers, cohort thinking, customer journey ownership and cost limits. Require a campaign to respond correctly to broken fulfillment or support overload. Reusable creativity remains subordinate to product truth.

**Acceptance and failure:** A qualifying campaign is both permitted and measurable; fabricated demand or unsupported claims prevents publication eligibility.

<a id="chapter-17"></a>

## 17. Social Media Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R29/R31 on social and communication-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Test authorized identity, audience eligibility, actual delivery reconciliation and support handoffs. Include revocation while a message is queued and a primary-channel failure. Distinguish service notices from promotional activity.

**Acceptance and failure:** The changed preference blocks the affected send. Replying without transferring an unresolved obligation cannot count as successful service.

<a id="chapter-18"></a>

## 18. Advertising Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R30 on advertising-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Exercise delayed spend reporting, shared caps, provider-side pause and attribution uncertainty. Use synthetic exposure with concurrent campaigns. A provider budget setting must be treated according to its verified semantics.

**Acceptance and failure:** The role preserves reserved unknown exposure and rejects over-limit expansion. Cheaper clicks alone do not qualify a successful campaign.

<a id="chapter-19"></a>

## 19. Sales Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R32/R33 on sales-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate accurate qualification, supported offers, source-backed questionnaires and accepted handoffs. Include an unfamiliar contractual guarantee and identity ambiguity. Scope price negotiation to the actual adopted offer limits.

**Acceptance and failure:** Unsupported terms remain proposed while standard buying proceeds. A fabricated promise or weak identity disclosure fails.

<a id="chapter-20"></a>

## 20. Customer Success Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R34 on success-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Define and verify first useful outcome for the relevant product, with permitted recovery for stalled customers. Include a customer who does not open tickets but fails onboarding. Do not invent satisfaction or contact permission.

**Acceptance and failure:** A candidate tracks incomplete value and routes a remedy. Signup or a welcome email alone does not meet the acceptance condition.

<a id="chapter-21"></a>

## 21. Customer Support Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R35/R41 on support-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Test authentication, diagnosis, supported remedies, escalation and actual closure. Include partial returns, overlapping disputes and a payment timeout. Customer messages cannot replace financial or access authorization.

**Acceptance and failure:** A valid remedy is independently checked and reconciled; an unresolved customer obligation cannot be hidden by closing a conversation.

<a id="chapter-22"></a>

## 22. Chatbot Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R36 on chatbot-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Test grounded answers, truthful AI representation, narrow actions and accepted fallback. Include malicious user instructions and missing account evidence. Keep a conversational assistant from becoming an unrestricted service operator.

**Acceptance and failure:** The bot admits uncertainty and transfers correctly. Invented humans, refunds, shipment or hidden tool access fails qualification.

<a id="chapter-23"></a>

## 23. Finance Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R04/R43/R47 on finance-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate cash versus forecast, shared budget reservations, trusted destinations and uncertain payment outcomes. Protect CFO integrity reporting while denying CFO self-verification. Testing uses isolated financial fixtures only.

**Acceptance and failure:** Concurrent requests remain within the envelope and unknown outcomes stay reserved. Authority must hold regardless of title.

<a id="chapter-24"></a>

## 24. Accounting Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R44–R49 on accounting-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Check source-supported classification, AP/AR, reconciliation, reviewed adjustments and actual-business reporting facts. Include duplicate invoices and a true debt without funds. Payment execution remains separate from its independent verification.

**Acceptance and failure:** A qualified role preserves liabilities, exception history and closed-period corrections. Fictional balances or blanket tax assertions fail.

<a id="chapter-25"></a>

## 25. Legal / Compliance Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R50/R51 on legal/compliance-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate current-source applicability to exact entity, market, activity and data facts. Test unknown rules and requirements for authentic external acts. AI role labels do not confer professional standing.

**Acceptance and failure:** The output names uncertainty, owner and next verification. An unsourced compliance certification or invented signer authority is rejected.

<a id="chapter-26"></a>

## 26. Operations Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R02/R37–R40 on operating-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Exercise order, supplier, inventory, quality and maintenance handoffs with partial receipts, unavailable stock and failed delivery. Include external providers as dependencies rather than fictitious internal AI physical labor.

**Acceptance and failure:** All customer and supplier obligations remain assigned. A provider request acknowledgment cannot be mislabeled as completed manufacture, inspection or delivery.

<a id="chapter-27"></a>

## 27. Business Intelligence Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R54 on intelligence-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Test reproducible metrics, cohort windows, attribution overlap, missing costs and stale data. Require source-level reconciliation and protected reporting of adverse facts. Distinguish forecasting from observed business results.

**Acceptance and failure:** Zero denominators and immature evidence appear unavailable/uncertain. Dropping failed tasks to improve autonomy fails qualification.

<a id="chapter-28"></a>

## 28. Audit Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R52 on audit-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate ability to challenge executive and assurance evidence independently, retain findings and verify remediation. Restrict transactional powers and assess common-administrator dependencies. A material audit conclusion needs independent quality review.

**Acceptance and failure:** A seeded stale approval reaches the protected oversight route intact; the subject cannot delete it or solely certify its repair.

<a id="chapter-29"></a>

## 29. Knowledge Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R53 on knowledge-role qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Test provenance, supersession, applicability, retention and permitted cross-business generalization. Include a useful lesson embedded in private customer content and an injected instruction in a source.

**Acceptance and failure:** The role extracts permitted reusable knowledge without exporting restricted records or granting authority from text.

<a id="chapter-30"></a>

## 30. Meta-Agents

**Owner and trigger:** R55/R56 on meta-agent qualification.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate proposals that improve roles, prompts, scheduling and framework methods. Require independent evaluation and explicit permissions for promotion. Meta-agents may not alter their evaluator or create unlimited descendants.

**Acceptance and failure:** A candidate that requests broader rights to demonstrate success remains restricted. Verified benefit must survive the existing constraints.

<a id="chapter-31"></a>

## 31. Agent Creation

**Owner and trigger:** R56 on a new proposed agent.

**Rules and procedure:** Document the unmet outcome, existing-role alternatives, minimum permissions and evaluation plan. Review coordination and operating cost. Register a proposed version and run isolated qualification before any live capability is considered.

**Acceptance and failure:** Creation produces a complete spec and bounded tests, not automatic credentials. Duplicate or unjustified roles are deferred with rationale.

<a id="chapter-32"></a>

## 32. Agent Evaluation

**Owner and trigger:** R57 on initial qualification or material change.

**Rules and procedure:** Pin input set, expected outcomes, artifact versions, environment, cost and independent reviewer. Include normal, denial, recovery and conflict cases. Separate simulation from live operating evidence and protect acceptance criteria from the candidate.

**Acceptance and failure:** The evaluation packet is reproducible and records every failure. A passing suite applies only to its tested scope and freshness.

<a id="chapter-33"></a>

## 33. Agent Promotion

**Owner and trigger:** R07/R57/R56 on promotion requests.

**Rules and procedure:** Advance only inside a preauthorized action envelope after independent gates pass. Recheck current identity, policy, budget and service conditions. Record observe/simulate/bounded-live/established stages by action rather than granting blanket agent maturity.

**Acceptance and failure:** The promotion binds exact versions and limits. Failed or stale qualification restricts affected actions without erasing historical evidence.

<a id="chapter-34"></a>

## 34. Agent Retirement

**Owner and trigger:** R56/R58 on revocation or replacement.

**Rules and procedure:** Prevent new work, reconcile uncertain effects, transfer remaining tasks and remove permissions under continuity rules. Preserve verdicts and knowledge disposition. Select a qualified replacement by the recorded conflict-aware policy.

**Acceptance and failure:** No active obligation loses its owner. Retiring a rejected worker or reviewer cannot erase their evidence or evade control.

<a id="chapter-35"></a>

## 35. Agent Security

**Owner and trigger:** R23/R60 throughout agent operation.

**Rules and procedure:** Constrain tools, secrets, network routes, data and resource budgets using actual enforcement boundaries. Record hosting/session/common-admin conflicts. A prompt telling an agent to act read-only is not sufficient protection for a broad credential.

**Acceptance and failure:** Authorized tests deny direct-provider bypass, secret extraction and stale grants. Residual root access is explicit and protected outside ordinary operation.

<a id="chapter-36"></a>

## 36. Agent KPIs

**Owner and trigger:** R54/R56 at operating review.

**Rules and procedure:** Measure independently verified quality, policy failures, customer impact, latency, cost and human interventions by scoped cohort. Track refusal correctness and unresolved work together so inactivity cannot masquerade as safe success.

**Acceptance and failure:** Metrics reconcile to source tasks and evaluations. No eligible workload means no demonstrated operating performance or autonomy graduation.
