# Dashboards, Analytics & KPIs — Complete Chapter Manual

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft manuscript; adoption and operational activation pending

## Reading and authority

This manual supplies substantive procedures for every chapter in the selected blueprint. The [constitution](../../CONSTITUTION.md) is canonical for all 60 roles, reporting, authority and protected oversight. “Company” means the relevant actual business; Unilogistix itself is the reusable framework. LondonRue towels is an example, not an inferred formation or launch.

Each chapter names its owner and trigger, required procedure, and acceptance/failure evidence. All work inherits the constitutional mandate, independent review, privacy, bounded recovery and truthful outcome requirements. Numeric limits and real account/entity identities belong to the [business profile](../../templates/business-instance.md); missing required authority restricts the affected action. Documentary completeness does not claim adopted rules or implemented controls. Every operational human ask is a failure and must be recorded without concealing unresolved work.

Follow [operating assurance](../../policies/operating-assurance.md), [watchdog escalation](../../policies/watchdogs-and-repair-alerts.md), [decision rights](../../governance/DECISION_RIGHTS.md) and [business-to-framework learning](../../workflows/business-to-framework-learning.md) where applicable. A real legal/provider requirement is verified for its exact business, action and current source before use; no illustrative company cadence is a statutory deadline.

## 01. Executive Command Center

**Owner and trigger:** R54/R01 on executive reporting.

**Rules and procedure:** Show authority, capability, operations, economics and learning separately. Prioritize changed facts, critical incidents, unowned/overdue obligations and actual decisions. Link summaries to scoped source records; do not replace evidence with agent narration.

**Acceptance and failure:** A viewer can trace a material claim and its freshness. Unknown or conflicting data is visible and creates an owned correction.

<a id="chapter-02"></a>

## 02. Founder Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R07/R54 for founder oversight.

**Rules and procedure:** Present genuine strategic/authority decisions, current risks, adopted limits and verified business outcomes. Provide protected audit and integrity findings without executive filtering. Routine incident visibility must not become a demand that the founder perform repairs.

**Acceptance and failure:** Each requested decision has exact scope, alternatives, cost and deadline basis. Overdue proposals remain undecided, not implicitly approved.

<a id="chapter-03"></a>

## 03. CEO Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R54 for business coordination.

**Rules and procedure:** Display outcomes by mandate, owner and obligation with customer impact, resource usage and dependencies. Separate business status from framework development. Include failed and blocked operations and accepted recovery actions.

**Acceptance and failure:** The executive cannot improve the dashboard by dropping failures or hiding adverse verification. Decisions link to actual follow-through owners.

<a id="chapter-04"></a>

## 04. Company Health Score

**Owner and trigger:** R54 when proposing a composite health indicator.

**Rules and procedure:** A composite is optional and must disclose component definitions and weights. Hard failures, security/privacy breaches and unmet obligations remain independently visible and cannot be averaged away. Never use one score as proof of autonomy or viability.

**Acceptance and failure:** Validate the score against component data and decision usefulness. If aggregation masks a material failure, remove or revise it.

<a id="chapter-05"></a>

## 05. Product Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R06/R54 on product review.

**Rules and procedure:** Show defined first-value outcomes, supported availability, defects/returns, retention and open product obligations. For goods, distinguish placed orders, shipment and verified fulfillment; for software, define actual useful service events.

**Acceptance and failure:** Each measure links its eligible cohort and source. Login or payment alone cannot falsely prove product value.

<a id="chapter-06"></a>

## 06. Engineering Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R03/R54 on engineering review.

**Rules and procedure:** Report accepted lead time, escaped defects, change failure and maintenance exposure by artifact and business. Include blocked work and relevant test coverage; avoid raw code or commit counts as success targets.

**Acceptance and failure:** Sampled releases reconcile to checks and observed outcomes. Missing deployment evidence prevents a deployed-status claim.

<a id="chapter-07"></a>

## 07. QA Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R21/R54 on quality review.

**Rules and procedure:** Measure material defects detected, escaped issues, test reliability and acceptance gaps. Pair speed with false-positive/negative impact. Track skipped and flaky checks separately; test count and coverage percentage cannot replace risk coverage.

**Acceptance and failure:** QA status names exact tested versions and exclusions. Known failing gates cannot be summarized as green.

<a id="chapter-08"></a>

## 08. Security Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R23/R54 on risk changes and incidents.

**Rules and procedure:** Display material exposures, credential/control anomalies, remediation age and qualification gaps with restricted evidence access. Keep critical alerts prominent and independent of an operating manager’s desired status.

**Acceptance and failure:** Findings link to affected scope and verification. Stale or self-certified closure is rejected and escalated.

<a id="chapter-09"></a>

## 09. DevOps Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R24/R26/R54 on service operations.

**Rules and procedure:** Show customer-path availability, capacity, deployment failures, recovery evidence and maintenance deadlines. Distinguish host health from actual service. Include missed monitoring and dependent provider state.

**Acceptance and failure:** Availability uses defined eligible events and observation windows. Absent monitoring is unknown, not perfect uptime.

<a id="chapter-10"></a>

## 10. AI Operations Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R56/R54 on agent operation.

**Rules and procedure:** Report verified completion, denied/unsafe actions, recovery, cost and latency by action/role version. Show observe/simulate/live qualification separately from mandate. Include unfinished cohorts and manual interventions.

**Acceptance and failure:** No workload means no demonstrated autonomy. A new model cannot inherit an established score without applicable current evidence.

<a id="chapter-11"></a>

## 11. Marketing Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R05/R27/R54 on campaigns.

**Rules and procedure:** Track qualified acquisition, activation/fulfillment, retention and contribution alongside spend, complaints and capacity constraints. Use declared attribution and mature cohorts. Avoid impressions as the sole measure of value.

**Acceptance and failure:** Campaign figures reconcile to customer and finance sources. Overlapping channel credit is disclosed rather than summed into duplicate customers.

<a id="chapter-12"></a>

## 12. Social Media Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R29/R54 on social results.

**Rules and procedure:** Show relevant engagement, verified publication, supported referrals and accepted service handoffs. Separate bots/test/internal activity where identifiable and permitted. Preserve failed posts, corrections and unanswered customer cases.

**Acceptance and failure:** A published count requires provider evidence. Fabricated engagement or ignored support messages cannot improve performance.

<a id="chapter-13"></a>

## 13. Sales Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R32/R54 on sales pipeline.

**Rules and procedure:** Define opportunity stages, eligibility, accepted offers and fulfilled/activated sales. Calculate conversion using a declared cohort; show aging, losses and unsupported-term requests. Forecast deals separately from actual receipts.

**Acceptance and failure:** A quoted or signed opportunity is not automatically fulfilled revenue. Source evidence determines the reported stage.

<a id="chapter-14"></a>

## 14. Customer Success Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R34/R54 on customer value.

**Rules and procedure:** Measure time to first value, completion of onboarding, retention and outstanding commitments by product. Track stalled customers with uncertainty rather than assuming silence means success.

**Acceptance and failure:** Success requires a product-specific useful event. Missing or delayed evidence remains an incomplete outcome with an owner.

<a id="chapter-15"></a>

## 15. Customer Support Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R35/R54 on service quality.

**Rules and procedure:** Report response, verified resolution and reopening by severity/cohort; show backlog age and owner. Keep incident grouping from hiding individual obligations. Pair speed with correctness and repeat complaints.

**Acceptance and failure:** Ticket closure must link remedy evidence. A reply-only closure or suppressed complaint is a reporting failure.

<a id="chapter-16"></a>

## 16. Finance Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R04/R54 on financial review.

**Rules and procedure:** Separate reconciled cash, reservations, liabilities, forecast and available discretionary capacity. Show budget variance, committed recurring exposure and funding for existing obligations. Protected integrity exceptions remain visible.

**Acceptance and failure:** Cash-available calculations use adopted reserves without double counting. Unknown settlements remain exposure, not free funds.

<a id="chapter-17"></a>

## 17. Accounting Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R44/R48/R54 at close and reconciliation.

**Rules and procedure:** Show subledger/bank differences, unsupported entries, adjustment review, overdue close work and financial statement freshness. Retain closed-period corrections and known accounting uncertainty.

**Acceptance and failure:** Each reported balance reconciles to source records and the adopted method. An unexplained plug entry cannot make a close complete.

<a id="chapter-18"></a>

## 18. Operations Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R02/R37/R54 on physical/service delivery.

**Rules and procedure:** Report order accuracy, fulfillment time, stock discrepancies, supplier quality and overdue recurring work. Define the denominator by relevant accepted orders or obligations, including failed and partial outcomes.

**Acceptance and failure:** Carrier request acceptance is separate from delivery. No missing order disappears from the cohort to improve service reliability.

<a id="chapter-19"></a>

## 19. Legal / Compliance Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R50/R51/R54 on obligations and requirements.

**Rules and procedure:** Show applicable duties, owner, source date, due rule, evidence and unresolved facts by actual business. Distinguish proposed policy, implemented control and verified effectiveness. Keep privileged records access-scoped.

**Acceptance and failure:** A document heading or planned control cannot be shown as compliance. Unknown applicability remains a visible gate.

<a id="chapter-20"></a>

## 20. Research Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R09/R54 on research investment.

**Rules and procedure:** Show decision questions resolved, source freshness, uncertainty and forecast calibration against later evidence. Record cost and rejected hypotheses. Avoid paper/source volume as the outcome.

**Acceptance and failure:** A research metric traces to an actual decision or validated question. Invented interviews or selective evidence invalidate the result.

<a id="chapter-21"></a>

## 21. Agent Performance

**Owner and trigger:** R56/R57/R54 on role evaluation.

**Rules and procedure:** Measure quality, authority errors, cost, latency and recurrence by exact configuration and action. Include denial correctness and unavailable workloads. Separate independent review from self-ratings.

**Acceptance and failure:** Comparisons use compatible cases and declared scope. Role names and subjective confidence cannot substitute for performance evidence.

<a id="chapter-22"></a>

## 22. AI Cost

**Owner and trigger:** R43/R54 on model/tool expenditure.

**Rules and procedure:** Define cost per accepted outcome as attributable total model/tool cost divided by independently accepted outcomes over a matched cohort. Include retries and review costs; distinguish marginal invoices from allocated infrastructure.

**Acceptance and failure:** Zero accepted outcomes yields unavailable or explicit total loss, not zero cost per success. Missing usage is flagged for reconciliation.

<a id="chapter-23"></a>

## 23. Workflow Performance

**Owner and trigger:** R08/R54 on workflow review.

**Rules and procedure:** Track latency, handoff rejection, deadlines, duplication and autonomous verified completion using all eligible started work. Preserve child/parent lineage and shared costs to prevent task-splitting distortion.

**Acceptance and failure:** A workflow score must reconcile to outcomes and open obligations. Dropped failures or arbitrary denominator changes fail validation.

<a id="chapter-24"></a>

## 24. Revenue Analytics

**Owner and trigger:** R44/R54 on commercial reporting.

**Rules and procedure:** Define net revenue under the adopted accounting policy and separate it from gross orders, collected cash, refunds and forecasts. Attribute cohorts consistently and state recognition/time assumptions.

**Acceptance and failure:** Figures reconcile to invoices, adjustments and ledger records. A payment receipt alone does not determine revenue treatment.

<a id="chapter-25"></a>

## 25. Profitability

**Owner and trigger:** R04/R54 on product and cohort economics.

**Rules and procedure:** Define contribution before acquisition and after consistently allocated acquisition costs. Include relevant goods, fulfillment, support, returns and infrastructure without double counting. Display incomplete cost estimates and immature cohorts.

**Acceptance and failure:** Loss-making growth triggers a permitted review of future exposure while owed customer service continues. Fictional margins cannot justify expansion.

<a id="chapter-26"></a>

## 26. Customer Analytics

**Owner and trigger:** R51/R54 on customer analysis.

**Rules and procedure:** Use permitted data to examine activation, retention, remedies and service burden with minimal necessary identity. Keep business boundaries and purpose restrictions. Avoid intrusive profiling or unjustified cross-business joins.

**Acceptance and failure:** Aggregates retain denominator and provenance. Unauthorized data use invalidates the analysis even if predictive accuracy improves.

<a id="chapter-27"></a>

## 27. Market Intelligence

**Owner and trigger:** R09/R54 when market conditions change.

**Rules and procedure:** Summarize dated external signals, demand evidence and alternative explanations. Separate measured change from assumptions about total market size. Connect each signal to a bounded business decision.

**Acceptance and failure:** Unverified estimates carry uncertainty and source dates. They cannot become guarantees of demand or investment authority.

<a id="chapter-28"></a>

## 28. Competitive Intelligence

**Owner and trigger:** R09/R54 on competitor comparisons.

**Rules and procedure:** Use current permitted evidence for products, prices, claims and alternatives. Distinguish marketing assertions from independently supported facts and protect confidential sources.

**Acceptance and failure:** A comparison records limitations and recheck triggers. Stale claims are removed from customer-facing materials.

<a id="chapter-29"></a>

## 29. Predictive Analytics

**Owner and trigger:** R54/R10 on forecasts.

**Rules and procedure:** Document prediction target, horizon, input scope, assumptions, uncertainty and validation against later outcomes. Compare to simple baselines and watch drift. Forecasts inform permitted decisions but never create cash, authority or guaranteed value.

**Acceptance and failure:** Track forecast versus actual without rewriting the past. Poor calibration triggers restriction or redesign.

<a id="chapter-30"></a>

## 30. Risk Dashboard

**Owner and trigger:** R07/R23/R54 on risk review.

**Rules and procedure:** Show individual material risks, control owner, current evidence, residual exposure and deadlines. Keep common-mode failures and dependencies visible. Aggregation must not hide mandatory unmet safeguards.

**Acceptance and failure:** A risk status requires evidence and acceptance authority. A green overall score cannot neutralize a known prohibited action.

<a id="chapter-31"></a>

## 31. Daily Executive Briefing

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R54 daily by proposed default.

**Rules and procedure:** Summarize changed facts, customer/service impact, cash exposure, failed/overdue work and next actions. Link critical incidents and genuine decisions. Avoid repeating unchanged narrative at the expense of actionable exceptions.

**Acceptance and failure:** The briefing has clear owners and freshness. Ordinary tasks remain with AI operations rather than becoming founder approvals.

<a id="chapter-32"></a>

## 32. Weekly Review

**Owner and trigger:** R01/R07 weekly by proposed default.

**Rules and procedure:** Review outcome trends, recurring defects, resource allocation, commitments and learning disposition. Compare plans to observed results and assign bounded corrective work. Surface protected audit and finance findings intact.

**Acceptance and failure:** Each material finding receives a decision or owner/date. A meeting or report alone does not close the underlying issue.

<a id="chapter-33"></a>

## 33. Monthly Business Review

**Owner and trigger:** R04/R01 monthly after the adopted close process.

**Rules and procedure:** Review reconciled accounts, product economics, retention/service, forecasts and funded obligations. Identify scope changes needing actual authority separately from routine optimization.

**Acceptance and failure:** The review cites close freshness and unresolved financial exceptions. It cannot silently treat estimated balances as reconciled.

<a id="chapter-34"></a>

## 34. Quarterly Strategic Review

**Owner and trigger:** R07/R01 quarterly by proposed default.

**Rules and procedure:** Assess mission fit, business viability, portfolio constraints, governance, autonomy and long-term dependencies. Keep strategy hypotheses distinct from commitments; require exact adoption for new authority.

**Acceptance and failure:** Decisions include alternatives, costs and review dates. Existing valid operations continue without inventing a new budget.

<a id="chapter-35"></a>

## 35. KPI Governance

**Owner and trigger:** R54/R07 when metrics are created or changed.

**Rules and procedure:** Register ID, owner, purpose, formula, unit, source, window, exclusions, freshness, target and breach action. Version changes and preserve historical comparability. Independently review incentives and material reporting changes.

**Acceptance and failure:** A metric cannot be altered merely to turn a failure green. Broken provenance or undefined denominator blocks decision-grade use.

<a id="chapter-36"></a>

## 36. Data Quality

**Owner and trigger:** R18/R54 on ingestion or reconciliation failures.

**Rules and procedure:** Check completeness, duplicates, timeliness, referential integrity and business-state agreement. Assign source corrections rather than silently editing reports. Keep unavailable status while material defects remain.

**Acceptance and failure:** Sample source-to-view reconciliation and deliberate bad data tests establish scoped quality. A successful refresh job alone is insufficient.

<a id="chapter-37"></a>

## 37. Dashboard Security

**Owner and trigger:** R51/R60/R54 on dashboard access and exports.

**Rules and procedure:** Authorize by business, role and purpose; minimize personal/financial detail and protect discrepancy reporting. Apply restrictions to downloads and cached views, not only navigation menus.

**Acceptance and failure:** Unauthorized business/customer access and exports are denied in later tests. An attractive interface cannot substitute for enforced data boundaries.

<a id="chapter-38"></a>

## 38. Continuous Improvement

**Owner and trigger:** R55/R54 after reporting or decision failures.

**Rules and procedure:** Turn misleading measures, missed alerts and useful analytical methods into validated improvements. Compare decision quality and cost before promotion; preserve historical definitions and privacy. Distribute compatible changes through framework CI/CD.

**Acceptance and failure:** A dashboard improvement must improve trustworthy decisions, not merely visual appeal. Regression reopens the linked lesson and restores a known accepted view.
