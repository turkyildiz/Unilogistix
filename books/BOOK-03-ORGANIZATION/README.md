# Organization & Departments

Version: 0.2 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Initial specification; runtime not implemented

## Organization model

Founder → AI executive accountability → department responsibilities → specialist execution. Departments are interfaces and ownership boundaries; instantiate agents only when useful work requires them.

The CEO owns portfolio coordination; CTO owns technical architecture and engineering; COO owns reliable business operations; CFO owns financial administration within limits; CMO owns truthful growth and acquisition.

## Functional coverage

Product and research own customer problems, requirements, market and competitive evidence. Engineering covers architecture, frontend, backend, data, AI/ML, UX/UI, QA, security, and DevOps/SRE.

Growth covers brand, marketing, social media, advertising, sales, and CRM. Customer operations cover onboarding, success, support, chatbot operations, and order tracking. Finance covers budgets, billing, accounting, reconciliation, and reporting.

Legal/compliance coordination identifies requirements and evidence needs; an agent title does not establish professional credentials or authority to bind an entity. Internal audit independently reviews control evidence. Knowledge management owns provenance and retirement. Business intelligence owns decision-oriented metrics. Program management owns cross-department dependencies.

## Department contract

Use the [department template](../../templates/department.md). Each department defines mission, lead role, agents, inputs, outputs, tools, workflows, KPIs, budget reference, permissions, escalation, and review cadence.

Handoffs use explicit acceptance criteria. Product cannot close engineering work without accepted deliverables; marketing cannot publish unsupported performance claims; finance cannot treat a sales forecast as collected revenue.

## Staffing and separation

One model or runtime may implement multiple roles. Material review must run in a distinct execution context with the required evidence and independent permission boundary. Role labels alone do not create independence.

Activate the smallest useful team for the current objective; account for coordination and model cost. A role can be dormant without leaving its responsibilities unowned.

## Acceptance

For a sample product change, identify one accountable owner at every handoff, all required reviews, and an escalation route. Demonstrate that no task is orphaned and no responsibility implies ungranted tool access.

## Autonomous operation requirement

Every operational human ask is a failure. Apply [the founder-directed autonomy policy](../../policies/autonomy.md), with AI recovery, honest failure accounting, and no authority bypass. Routine escalations go to AI functions; reserved board decisions remain explicit.
