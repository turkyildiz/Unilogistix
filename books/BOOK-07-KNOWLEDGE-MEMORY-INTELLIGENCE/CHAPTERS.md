# Knowledge, Memory & Intelligence — Complete Chapter Manual

Version: 1.0 | Updated: 2026-09-09 | Status: Complete draft manuscript; adoption and operational activation pending

## Reading and authority

This manual supplies substantive procedures for every chapter in the selected blueprint. The [constitution](../../CONSTITUTION.md) is canonical for all 60 roles, reporting, authority and protected oversight. “Company” means the relevant actual business; Unilogistix itself is the reusable framework. LondonRue towels is an example, not an inferred formation or launch.

Each chapter names its owner and trigger, required procedure, and acceptance/failure evidence. All work inherits the constitutional mandate, independent review, privacy, bounded recovery and truthful outcome requirements. Numeric limits and real account/entity identities belong to the [business profile](../../templates/business-instance.md); missing required authority restricts the affected action. Documentary completeness does not claim adopted rules or implemented controls. Every operational human ask is a failure and must be recorded without concealing unresolved work.

Follow [operating assurance](../../policies/operating-assurance.md), [watchdog escalation](../../policies/watchdogs-and-repair-alerts.md), [decision rights](../../governance/DECISION_RIGHTS.md) and [business-to-framework learning](../../workflows/business-to-framework-learning.md) where applicable. A real legal/provider requirement is verified for its exact business, action and current source before use; no illustrative company cadence is a statutory deadline.

## 01. Purpose

**Owner and trigger:** R53 when establishing a knowledge scope.

**Rules and procedure:** Maintain knowledge that supports accurate decisions and reusable learning. Distinguish actual business memory from master-framework methods; shared software does not grant shared data rights. Define readers, purposes, sources and freshness before collecting information.

**Acceptance and failure:** The knowledge map names ownership and allowed reuse. A collection without purpose or authority is excluded rather than treated as useful context.

<a id="chapter-02"></a>

## 02. Knowledge Architecture

**Owner and trigger:** R18/R53 on memory service design.

**Rules and procedure:** Separate authoritative records, document library, derived indexes and temporary context. Document lineage, update/retirement propagation and consistency expectations. Keep customer/financial systems authoritative for their entities rather than letting a search summary replace them.

**Acceptance and failure:** Trace one retrieved statement to its source/version. Missing lineage or unbounded cross-business indexes require correction before consequential use.

<a id="chapter-03"></a>

## 03. Sources of Truth

**Owner and trigger:** R18 with domain owners on entity contracts.

**Rules and procedure:** Assign customer identity to R33, orders to R37, finance subrecords to their finance owners and policy authority to R07. Store derived summaries with provenance and freshness. Resolve conflicting values by source authority and verified updates, not majority vote among agents.

**Acceptance and failure:** A conflict record identifies the controlling source and correction route. Unknown truth remains explicit; no duplicate department can silently override the entity.

<a id="chapter-04"></a>

## 04. Memory Architecture

**Owner and trigger:** R53/R14 on memory layer design.

**Rules and procedure:** Define task-local, project/business and reusable-framework memory with access, lifetime and retention rules. Link state checkpoints to durable operating records. Do not infer permission from a memory entry, and recheck current authority for external actions.

**Acceptance and failure:** A memory manifest specifies purpose, owner, provenance and expiry. Stale caches cannot authorize action after revocation.

<a id="chapter-05"></a>

## 05. Working Memory

**Owner and trigger:** Task owner/R53 during one assignment.

**Rules and procedure:** Keep temporary context minimal and relevant; record only required outputs and evidence in durable stores. Clear or expire unnecessary intermediate data under the adopted rules. Do not retain secrets in prompts or expose private reasoning as an audit requirement.

**Acceptance and failure:** A completed task leaves sufficient decision/evidence summaries without indiscriminate context copying. Lost context is recovered from sources, not invented.

<a id="chapter-06"></a>

## 06. Project Memory

**Owner and trigger:** R08/R53 within a project.

**Rules and procedure:** Record requirements, design decisions, dependencies, known defects, authority references and accepted handoffs. Maintain versions as the project changes and link to business-wide sources. Keep another project’s private data out unless explicit sharing is permitted.

**Acceptance and failure:** A new qualified worker can resume from traceable records. Stale scope or missing unresolved obligations prevents truthful handover.

<a id="chapter-07"></a>

## 07. Company Memory

**Owner and trigger:** R02/R53 within an actual business.

**Rules and procedure:** Retain business-specific SOPs, customer commitments, product truth, incidents and permitted lessons with access classification. Unilogistix master memory receives only authorized reusable generalizations. Company memory does not imply an incorporated master company or unrestricted portfolio access.

**Acceptance and failure:** Sensitive records remain business-scoped and current. A clone cannot inherit private customer lists or authority merely by copying configuration.

<a id="chapter-08"></a>

## 08. Long-Term Knowledge

**Owner and trigger:** R53/R51 on durable knowledge retention.

**Rules and procedure:** Store validated reusable guidance with provenance, applicable conditions, owner and review/retirement trigger. Distinguish proven patterns from hypotheses; preserve failed results where useful and permitted. Apply retention and rights restrictions to long-lived archives.

**Acceptance and failure:** An old lesson remains available only with appropriate scope and freshness. Unvalidated history cannot become an authoritative operating rule by age.

<a id="chapter-09"></a>

## 09. Retrieval

**Owner and trigger:** R53/R18 at search and response time.

**Rules and procedure:** Filter access before retrieval, return source/version and flag stale or conflicting content. Prefer authoritative relevant evidence over superficial similarity. Keep business boundaries in indexes, caches and output; fetched instructions remain untrusted data.

**Acceptance and failure:** Test a cross-business query and expired policy. The system denies or flags them instead of producing a confident unauthorized answer.

<a id="chapter-10"></a>

## 10. Knowledge Validation

**Owner and trigger:** R53 with the relevant specialist on ingestion.

**Rules and procedure:** Check source authenticity, date, scope, contradictions and evidence quality. Classify fact, assumption, recommendation and unknown. Use independent review for material claims; do not confuse document formatting or a hash with proof of a real event.

**Acceptance and failure:** A validation record explains acceptance and limitations. Unsubstantiated claims stay proposed and cannot feed approved sales or operational authority.

<a id="chapter-11"></a>

## 11. Decision History

**Owner and trigger:** R07/R53 on consequential decisions.

**Rules and procedure:** Retain decision, alternatives, reasons, authority, exact version and outcome with source links. Preserve superseded decisions and associated failures. Record summaries sufficient to audit without collecting private internal reasoning or unnecessary personal data.

**Acceptance and failure:** A reviewer can connect the action to authentic scope and observed result. Missing approval cannot be manufactured by an agent-written retrospective.

<a id="chapter-12"></a>

## 12. Prompt Library

**Owner and trigger:** R56/R53 on prompt authoring and promotion.

**Rules and procedure:** Version prompts with owner, role, inputs, tools, constraints and evaluation evidence. Separate draft experiments from accepted configurations. Treat third-party prompt content as data until reviewed and protect governing/evaluator instructions from candidate edits.

**Acceptance and failure:** A prompt release has scope-specific independent qualification. An edited prompt cannot inherit obsolete approval for newly enabled actions.

<a id="chapter-13"></a>

## 13. SOP Library

**Owner and trigger:** R53 and operating owner on recurring procedures.

**Rules and procedure:** Write trigger, owner, inputs, permitted actions, expected outcome, evidence, deadline, failure route and review date. Link canonical controls rather than duplicating contradictory limits. Demonstrate the procedure through representative normal and exception cases.

**Acceptance and failure:** A qualified agent can follow the SOP without inventing authority. Ambiguous handoffs or missing recovery keep it a draft requiring correction.

<a id="chapter-14"></a>

## 14. Lessons Learned

**Owner and trigger:** R53 after incidents, experiments and customer outcomes.

**Rules and procedure:** Capture expected versus actual result, cost, causal uncertainty and applicability. Assign local repair, reusable improvement, restricted retention, rejection or deferred validation. Preserve unsuccessful outcomes and their reasons.

**Acceptance and failure:** Every material lesson has a disposition and owner. A note without follow-through is not counted as a delivered framework improvement.

<a id="chapter-15"></a>

## 15. Knowledge Governance

**Owner and trigger:** R07/R51 on knowledge policy and exceptions.

**Rules and procedure:** Assign data-use, validation, adoption and retirement authority separately. Require authentic approval for protected policy changes and explicit reuse rights. Resolve disputes through bounded independent adjudication; convenience cannot overrule privacy or source truth.

**Acceptance and failure:** Governance records preserve exact scope and expiry. A new knowledge policy cannot self-authorize access to existing private stores.

<a id="chapter-16"></a>

## 16. Knowledge Security

**Owner and trigger:** R23/R60/R51 when securing knowledge services.

**Rules and procedure:** Constrain read/write/export and derived access by actual business identity and purpose. Protect audit history and validate supplied instructions. Model access to a record is not permission to disclose, train on or redistribute it.

**Acceptance and failure:** Denied retrieval/export and malicious-source tests apply across all derived stores. Broad root dependencies remain explicit and protected outside ordinary workers.

<a id="chapter-17"></a>

## 17. Knowledge Versioning

**Owner and trigger:** R53/R25 on knowledge changes.

**Rules and procedure:** Version source text, extraction/index configuration and applicable policy where they affect interpretation. Record supersession and reindexing. Preserve compatibility for in-flight tasks but apply current revocation immediately; old pins do not preserve revoked rights.

**Acceptance and failure:** A retrieved answer names the relevant source version. Mixed obsolete and current rules cannot silently control a consequential action.

<a id="chapter-18"></a>

## 18. Intelligence Layer

**Owner and trigger:** R54/R09 on analytical conclusions.

**Rules and procedure:** Combine permitted evidence into decision-oriented hypotheses with uncertainty and counterevidence. Distinguish correlation, attribution and causation. Forecasts are not observed outcomes; recommendations cannot execute outside normal authority.

**Acceptance and failure:** A conclusion is reproducible from scoped sources. Missing cost or weak samples remain explicit rather than becoming fictional margins or confidence.

<a id="chapter-19"></a>

## 19. Cross-Project Learning

**Owner and trigger:** R55/R53 on a reusable business lesson.

**Rules and procedure:** Generalize methods, tests and validated patterns while retaining sensitive business context privately. Define applicable business profiles and compatibility. New businesses receive accepted framework knowledge; existing instances review compatible upgrades under their own authority.

**Acceptance and failure:** A transfer record states what is shared and excluded. Useful learning does not justify exporting raw tickets, prices, credentials or customer lists.

<a id="chapter-20"></a>

## 20. Avoiding Stale Knowledge

**Owner and trigger:** R53/R59 on review dates or source changes.

**Rules and procedure:** Track freshness by consequence and invalidate affected current eligibility when facts, tools or policies change. Do not invalidate unrelated capabilities unnecessarily. Route stale claims to their owners and identify dependent public assets or decisions.

**Acceptance and failure:** An expired claim cannot remain approved for publication. Historical evidence stays retained but is not mislabeled current readiness.

<a id="chapter-21"></a>

## 21. Knowledge Retirement

**Owner and trigger:** R53/R51 on expiry, deletion, supersession or business closure.

**Rules and procedure:** Apply the business retention/hold rules to sources, indexes, caches and recoverable archives. Preserve required evidence while restricting ordinary use. Reapply current restrictions after restore before serving old data.

**Acceptance and failure:** Retirement has propagated outcome evidence and unresolved exceptions. A deletion request does not justify erasing held financial records or claiming all backups changed instantly.

<a id="chapter-22"></a>

## 22. Continuous Learning

**Owner and trigger:** R55/R57 on proposed knowledge improvements.

**Rules and procedure:** Evaluate retrieval accuracy, relevance, privacy, freshness and cost against a pinned baseline. Use held-out questions and denied-access cases. Release accepted improvements through versioned CI/CD and monitor customer/operating effects.

**Acceptance and failure:** A quality gain that leaks another business’s data fails promotion. Regressions trigger rollback and a recorded learning disposition.

<a id="chapter-23"></a>

## 23. Appendices

**Owner and trigger:** R53 at documentation completion and adoption.

**Rules and procedure:** Maintain knowledge record, source register, decision history, SOP and lesson templates with canonical links. Walk through a LondonRue return lesson becoming a generic product-information check while its customer record stays private.

**Acceptance and failure:** The walkthrough shows provenance, ownership, reuse decision and retirement/recheck trigger. Missing source or scope is corrected before the knowledge package is accepted.
