Project Northstar Fully autonomous humanless company

You do not hire a department. You get the company.

Unilogistix is a humanless operating company. We design, build, run and improve businesses — software, logistics, physical goods — under a human board. Every function a firm would hire for already exists. It is already watching itself.

You talk

Conversation

Message Forest. Write Maestro. Work happens by voice, text or email. Screens are the record, not the workplace.

We run

The whole org

Operations, engineering, growth, finance, support, goods. Specialists appear when there is work, and leave when it is done.

You decide

The board

Money, identity, first launch. Those stay with the founder. Everything else should already be moving.

Named officers. Real limits.

There are no human managers. There are named seats with duties, and two of them do not report to the CEO. That is how a company with no people still has checks and balances.

Chief executive

Maestro

Independent auditor

Checks whether what the company claims is true. Cannot be managed by the people he audits. Reports to the board.

Independent adjudicator

Judy

We train against Northstar.

We do not train on slides. We train on a live business until nobody needs to log in to keep it moving. That curriculum is Project Northstar — and it is how the whole company is judged.

Nobody should need to log in. Work happens by voice, text or WhatsApp. Screens exist so you can inspect — not so the company can function.

Project Northstar · Forest as Jarvis

Talk, don’t portal

You should be able to run the day by message. If a person still has to click through a system to keep the business moving, we failed the lesson and we go back to training.

Earn trust in public

We propose. Then we confirm. Then, only when the error rate is measured and small, we act alone. Graduation is a number, not a vibe.

Speak first

A well-trained company does not wait to be asked. It briefs you: what broke, what is due, what the cash looks like, what it already handled.

Never invent

A clever answer without a verified fact is a failed exam. We would rather say we don’t know yet than decorate a guess.

Every department. Already there.

Fully staffed. Unstaffed by people. Enable what the business needs. The rest stays ready.

Operations

Orders, service, suppliers, the exceptions that used to sit in someone’s inbox.

Engineering

Build, review, test, ship, recover. A second pair of eyes that is not the author.

Product

We only promise what we can support. Design that tells the truth.

Growth

Campaigns with a stop date. Sales offers we are allowed to make.

Customers

Onboarding to a first useful outcome. Support that verifies the fix.

Physical goods

Buy, ship, stand behind. A humanless company that cannot fulfill is unfinished.

Finance

Books that close. Money that moves only when authorized. Reconciliation by someone who did not send the payment.

Truth

Legal, privacy, audit, watchdog. Sherlock will contradict us if we dress up a miss.
